# Social posts

Use for short posts and threads. Compress around one idea without turning it
into engagement bait.

## Single posts

1. Put the claim, observation, or useful surprise in the first line.
2. Keep only the context needed to understand or trust it.
3. Prefer concrete language over a broad lesson the evidence cannot support.
4. Let a strong statement end cleanly. Do not append a forced question or call
   to action.
5. Avoid manufactured hooks, suspense, outrage, and claims of universality.

## Threads

1. Use a thread only when the idea cannot remain clear and supported in one
   post.
2. Give each post one job and make the progression visible.
3. Do not repeat the opening claim in several forms to create length.
4. Put supporting detail next to the claim it supports.
5. End with the conclusion, implication, or next useful question rather than a
   recap of every post.

Keep the author's natural punctuation and rhythm. Short does not have to mean
flat, absolute, or polished into a slogan.
